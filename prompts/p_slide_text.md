====================================================================
WRITING STYLE
====================================================================

* Audience: social media scrollers with short attention spans. Lead with the big impact: this was a murder, not a normal death. What did they know, or how were they linked, that made them a target?
* A quick age reference often shows they did not die of old age. Add brief endearing details when they help (town, kids, job title and employer).
* Match the example's word count and bullet count. At most one or two extra bullets, only if truly needed.

# ELF Research Suppression

Extremely low frequency (ELF) electromagnetic research sits at the intersection of consciousness science, weapons development, and intelligence secrecy — a combination that has historically made its practitioners targets for harassment, discrediting, and worse.

[Timeline](timeline.mdx) | [By Method](methods.mdx) | [Intelligence Service Murders](index.mdx)

## What Is ELF Research?

Extremely low frequency (ELF) electromagnetic waves occupy the 3–30 Hz range of the electromagnetic spectrum. At frequencies corresponding to human brainwave states — particularly around 7–8 Hz (the Schumann resonance and theta brainwave range) — ELF waves have documented measurable effects on biological systems.

Key areas of research that attracted military and intelligence interest:

* **Consciousness entrainment** — the observation that external ELF fields can shift human brain states, affecting cognition, mood, and perceptual thresholds
* **Remote influence** — whether ELF broadcasts could alter behavior or mental states in a population without their awareness
* **Communication** — the U.S. Navy's Project ELF used 76 Hz transmissions to communicate with submerged submarines (Wisconsin/Michigan transmitters, operational 1989–2004)
* **Weaponization** — Soviet ELF weapons programs, allegedly based on Nikola Tesla's work, were a classified intelligence concern through the Cold War

The dual-use nature of this research — civilian medical applications on one side, weapons and mind-control applications on the other — created a classified environment in which independent researchers doing open publication posed a direct threat to state monopoly on the knowledge.

## Andrija Puharich — The Central Case

The most documented case of intelligence suppression of ELF research involves **Dr. Andrija Puharich** (1918–1995), an American physician and inventor who worked directly with U.S. intelligence for decades before becoming a target.

Puharich discovered that certain psychics produced an 8 Hz ELF wave during their most effective sessions, and that human brains could be trained via biofeedback to produce this frequency consciously. He believed this frequency was fundamental to human consciousness and psychic ability. He also alleged that the Soviet Union was testing ELF beam weapons capable of inducing disease, climatic disruption, and population-level behavior change.

In **August 1978**, his estate at Ossining, New York was burned to the ground by arson. Puharich publicly blamed the CIA. He subsequently fled to Mexico, describing himself as under active threat. The arson effectively ended his most productive research period.

## The Pattern: Why ELF Researchers Were Targeted

Intelligence agencies had compounding reasons to suppress independent ELF research:

**1. Offensive weapons exposure**
Published findings on ELF mind-control effects could alert adversaries to U.S. capabilities, reveal classified research programs, or constrain diplomatic options.

**2. Defensive vulnerability**
If an independent researcher proved Soviet ELF weapons were effective, it would create political and public pressure for responses the military establishment wasn't ready to make.

**3. Medical device monopoly**
ELF-based medical applications (brain stimulation, biofeedback therapy) represented substantial commercial and strategic value. Independent patents and publications threatened controlled development.

**4. Consciousness research classification**
The overlap between ELF research and psychic phenomena research (remote viewing, ESP) intersected with programs like MKULTRA and the CIA's Stargate program that were explicitly classified. Independent researchers doing adjacent open work were a containment problem.

## U.S. Government ELF Programs

The U.S. government ran significant classified ELF programs that provide context for why independent researchers were suppressed:

* **Project ELF (U.S. Navy)** — Submarine communication system using 76 Hz transmissions from transmitters in Wisconsin (Clam Lake) and Michigan (Republic). Operational 1989–2004. Opposed by anti-radiation activists for decades before its decommissioning.

* **CIA MKULTRA Subprojects** — Several MKULTRA subprojects investigated electromagnetic effects on behavior and consciousness. Declassified documents confirm funding for research into electromagnetic influence on human subjects, some conducted without consent.

* **Project Pandora (1960s)** — CIA/DoD investigation of the "Moscow Signal" — a microwave beam the Soviets had been directing at the U.S. Embassy in Moscow since at least 1953. Classified until 1976. Related to broader electromagnetic effects on human cognition.

* **Stargate Program** — CIA/DIA remote viewing program that ran 1972–1995, initially at SRI with Puharich-adjacent researchers (including Hal Puthoff and Russell Targ, who worked with Uri Geller under CIA funding).

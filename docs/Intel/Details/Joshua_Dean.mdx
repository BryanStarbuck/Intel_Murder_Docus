Quality auditor at Spirit AeroSystems, the company that builds the fuselages for Boeing's 737 MAX. He reported improperly drilled holes in a critical pressure structure, said management brushed it off, and said he was fired for raising too many problems. A healthy, athletic 45-year-old, he died of a sudden and extremely aggressive infection on May 1, 2024, less than two months after fellow Boeing whistleblower [John Barnett](John_Barnett.mdx) was found dead. No foul play has been officially indicated.

## Assessment: UNCERTAIN

Dean's death was sudden and shocking: a fit 45-year-old who went to the gym and ran regularly was dead roughly two weeks after first having trouble breathing. It came less than two months after [John Barnett](John_Barnett.mdx), another Boeing-related whistleblower represented by the same law firm, was found shot dead. That timing is the whole case for suspicion. The medical record points to a real, documented infection (influenza B plus antibiotic-resistant MRSA), and no autopsy finding or official body has indicated foul play. It belongs here because of the pattern, not because of forensic evidence.

## Who He Was

Josh Dean lived in the Wichita, Kansas area, where Spirit AeroSystems is based. He was a **third-generation aviation worker**: his father and grandfather had worked at the same factory before him.

He earned a degree in mechanical engineering and started at Spirit in 2019. He was laid off in 2020 during pandemic cuts, then came back in May 2021 as a **quality auditor** (some accounts describe the role as product or process verification). After leaving Spirit he worked briefly at Boeing's Wichita operation and then elsewhere.

His family says he lived a healthy life, going to the gym and running regularly, and was in generally good health before his final illness.

### What Spirit AeroSystems Is

Spirit AeroSystems is one of Boeing's most important suppliers. It builds the fuselage (the main body of the plane) and other major parts of the 737 MAX, then ships them to Boeing for final assembly. Problems at Spirit end up inside Boeing airplanes.

## What He Reported

### Misdrilled Holes in the Aft Pressure Bulkhead

The aft pressure bulkhead is a dome-shaped wall at the back of the passenger cabin. It is one of the structures that holds in the cabin's air pressure at altitude; a failure there can be catastrophic.

Dean said that holes in the bulkhead on 737 MAX fuselages were **drilled improperly or out of alignment**. He said he flagged this to multiple supervisors and managers starting around **October 2022**, called it one of the worst problems he had found, and that management did not deal with it properly.

### A Culture of Downplaying Defects

He alleged a wider culture of **under-counting or downplaying defects** under production pressure, and what he called **"serious and gross misconduct by senior quality management of the 737 production line."** These are his allegations.

### Later Confirmation

Spirit later publicly acknowledged related defects, including improperly drilled holes in the aft pressure bulkhead area. Those defects contributed to production pauses and heavy scrutiny after the **January 2024 Alaska Airlines 737 MAX 9 door-plug blowout**, when a panel tore out of a plane in flight.

Dean also gave **deposition testimony** in a shareholder lawsuit against Spirit, which alleged that the company withheld information about quality problems from investors.

## Firing and Retaliation Complaint

Spirit fired Dean in **April 2023**. According to Spirit, the reason was that he allegedly failed to catch a separate problem, faulty vertical tail-fin fittings, which led to delivery delays.

Dean said the firing was **retaliation for raising too many quality concerns**, and that it was meant to "send a message" to other workers.

He filed complaints with the **FAA**, and after he was fired, with the **Department of Labor**, alleging retaliation. That complaint was still pending when he died.

He was represented by the same law firm that represented [John Barnett](John_Barnett.mdx), including attorneys **Brian Knowles** and **Rob Turkewitz**.

## Circumstances of Death

In mid-to-late **April 2024**, Dean began having trouble breathing and was admitted to a hospital in Wichita. What happened next, as reported:

1. He tested positive for **influenza B** and **MRSA** (methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus*, a staph infection that resists common antibiotics).
2. He developed **severe pneumonia**. The infection spread aggressively through his **lungs and into his bloodstream**.
3. He was **intubated** (put on a breathing tube).
4. He was **airlifted to a hospital in Oklahoma City**.
5. He was placed on **ECMO** (extracorporeal membrane oxygenation), a machine that takes over for the heart and lungs by oxygenating the blood outside the body, used only in the most critical cases.
6. His kidneys failed, and he was put on **dialysis**.
7. A CT scan showed he had suffered a **stroke**.
8. In his final days the infection was so severe that doctors considered **amputating his extremities**.

He died on **May 1, 2024**, after about two weeks in critical condition.

### Family and Official Response

His mother, **Virginia Green**, and his aunt, **Carol Parsons**, spoke publicly about how sudden and severe his illness was, and how shocking it was given his health. His mother requested an autopsy. The family expressed confusion about how quickly he declined and asked for further examination.

Spirit AeroSystems expressed condolences and called the loss stunning. His attorneys described him as courageous for raising quality and safety issues.

Major reporting has not detailed any official autopsy findings beyond the reported diagnoses (flu, MRSA, pneumonia, stroke, and multi-organ failure). His death was treated as the result of the infection and its complications. **There is no official indication of foul play.**

## Why This Death Raises Questions

- He died **less than two months** after [John Barnett](John_Barnett.mdx), another Boeing-related whistleblower, was found shot dead during depositions against Boeing.
- Both men were represented by **the same law firm**.
- He was **45, fit, and healthy**, a regular at the gym and a runner, and was dead about two weeks after first having trouble breathing.
- His retaliation complaint was **still pending**, and he had given deposition testimony in a shareholder lawsuit alleging Spirit hid quality problems.
- He died in the middle of the most intense scrutiny the 737 MAX supply chain had faced since the two fatal 737 MAX crashes, following the Alaska Airlines door-plug blowout.
- His own family said they were confused by how fast he declined and requested an autopsy.

## Counterarguments / Official Explanation

- His illness is medically documented: positive tests for influenza B and MRSA, followed by pneumonia, bloodstream infection, stroke, and organ failure.
- Flu followed by MRSA pneumonia is a known and sometimes fatal combination, including in otherwise healthy adults.
- No autopsy finding or official body has indicated foul play.
- **Santiago Paredes**, another Spirit AeroSystems whistleblower, publicly dismissed the conspiracy claims.
- Major reporting and official accounts attribute his death to the infection.

## Public Reaction and Social Media

Coming so soon after Barnett's death, and in the middle of intense scrutiny of Boeing and Spirit, Dean's death set off widespread online speculation that the two deaths were linked, or that both men were silenced. Posts on X and elsewhere framed it as "Boeing whistleblowers dropping" or suggested foul play. **No evidence of foul play has been found, and neither Boeing nor Spirit has been accused by any official body of involvement in his death.**

The two deaths also prompted **more workers to come forward** with their own quality concerns.

### How Dean's Case Differs from Barnett's

Dean's allegations were about Spirit's production of 737 parts, not about Boeing's own assembly line, which was the focus of [John Barnett](John_Barnett.mdx)'s complaints about the 787. But both added to public and regulatory pressure on the 737 MAX and its supply chain.

Official records and news reporting attribute Dean's death to the documented infection and its complications. The speculation rests on the timing, his whistleblowing and Barnett's death, not on forensic evidence of homicide.

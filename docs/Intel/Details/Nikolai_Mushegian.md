## Assessment: SUSPICIOUS

A 29-year-old crypto millionaire publicly accuses the CIA, Mossad, and a "pedo elite" of running a Caribbean sex-trafficking blackmail ring — and predicts they will torture him to death. Hours later, he is found face-down in the surf, fully clothed, wallet still on him. Police closed the case as accidental within days. The tweet's specificity (named services, a "spy" ex-girlfriend, a planted laptop, framing) and the timing make this one of the most-discussed deaths in the cryptocurrency community.

## Circumstances of Death

In the early morning hours of October 28, 2022, at approximately 4:57 a.m. local time, Mushegian posted what would become his final tweet from his account [@delete_shitcoin](https://x.com/delete_shitcoin):

> "CIA and Mossad and pedo elite are running some kind of sex trafficking entrapment blackmail ring out of Puerto Rico and caribbean islands. They are going to frame me with a laptop planted by my ex gf who was a spy. They will torture me to death."

Reports indicate he left his residence walking toward the beach in the early morning. Hours later — by approximately 9:00 a.m. — his body was found in the surf at Condado / Ashford Beach in San Juan. According to police, he was fully clothed and his wallet was still on his person. The beach is publicly posted as having dangerous rip currents and strong waves; local advisories warn against swimming there.

San Juan police investigated and reported finding no evidence of foul play. The death was officially ruled an accidental drowning. According to family and authorities cited in press accounts, there was no indication of homicide.

## Background

Mushegian was an American computer scientist, software engineer, and early cryptocurrency pioneer. Born in Kentucky to Soviet immigrant scientists, he attended Carnegie Mellon University and went on to become a co-founder of **MakerDAO** — the decentralized finance protocol behind the **DAI** stablecoin, one of the largest and earliest decentralized stablecoins on Ethereum. He worked as CEO of an Ethereum development firm called Nexus and was a lead contributor to MakerDAO alongside co-founder Rune Christensen.

Mushegian designed and built the **Single-Collateral Dai (SCD)** system, launched in December 2017 as the first decentralized stablecoin on Ethereum. He was also credited with identifying smart-contract vulnerabilities following an early DAO hack and engineering key security features to protect MakerDAO. Reports also tie him to early work at Balancer Labs.

He spoke on panels alongside early Ethereum pioneers including Vitalik Buterin and Gavin Wood. He donated significantly to Carnegie Mellon for DeFi research — initially $1.3 million, later expanded with 10,000 Maker tokens valued at roughly $4.2 million at the time of donation. By the time of his death he was a wealthy crypto figure who divided his time between Florida and Puerto Rico, drawn in part by Puerto Rico's tax incentives for crypto and tech professionals.

Friends and acquaintances described him as reclusive but brilliant — an unusually private person in an industry of self-promoters.

## Intelligence Connections

Mushegian himself named the alleged intelligence services in his final public statement: **CIA**, **Mossad**, and an unnamed "pedo elite." His tweet specified an operational geography (Puerto Rico and the Caribbean), an alleged blackmail operation (sex-trafficking entrapment), an alleged framing method (a laptop planted by an ex-girlfriend he described as "a spy"), and a predicted outcome (torture leading to his death).

Independent reporting after his death repeatedly placed his social circle inside Puerto Rico's crypto scene — which centered on the same population of high-net-worth crypto figures who had relocated to the island for tax reasons. According to a May 2026 [post by @DissidentMedia](https://x.com/DissidentMedia/status/2053443730674180325), Mushegian was a friend of [Brock Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brock_Pierce), a Puerto Rico–based crypto billionaire who appeared in the documentary *An Open Secret* and whose released emails with Jeffrey Epstein discussed bringing "20 Ukrainian women" to Epstein. The post juxtaposes the headline of Mushegian's death with Pierce's Epstein correspondence and his presence at high-profile events. None of these juxtapositions have been investigated as evidence by any law-enforcement body, and the Puerto Rico authorities did not connect Mushegian's death to Pierce or any third party.

## Why This Death Raises Questions

- The final tweet is unusually specific: named intelligence services, a named alleged operation (sex-trafficking entrapment), a named geography (Puerto Rico and the Caribbean), a named framing method (planted laptop), and a predicted outcome (torture to death).
- The interval between the tweet and the body being found in the water was a matter of hours.
- Mushegian was reportedly fully clothed and still had his wallet — a detail more consistent with someone who walked into the water than someone who went swimming.
- The death was ruled an accidental drowning within a short timeframe and without a high-profile homicide investigation, despite a public statement on record predicting his murder.
- Mushegian moved in a Puerto Rico crypto social circle that has separately been the subject of public reporting tying participants to Jeffrey Epstein's correspondence.
- He had reportedly been openly worried about powerful enemies in the months prior to his death.
- The Condado/Ashford Beach area is genuinely known for dangerous rip currents — the official narrative has a plausible alternative explanation, which is the principal counterargument.

## Counterarguments and Alternative Explanations

- San Juan police investigated and found no evidence of foul play; the death was ruled accidental drowning.
- Family and friends, in some press accounts, acknowledged that Mushegian had publicly mentioned paranoia and mental-health struggles in the period leading up to his death.
- Condado / Ashford Beach is genuinely posted by local authorities as having dangerous rip currents and strong waves; local warnings advise against swimming there. An accidental drowning is consistent with the geography.
- No declassified document, court finding, or official inquiry has linked Mushegian's death to any intelligence service.
- The framing of his death as intelligence-linked rests primarily on his own statements, which are alarming and specific but unverified.

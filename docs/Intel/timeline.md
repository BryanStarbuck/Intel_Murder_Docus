## 1930s

- **1935** — [Huey Long](Details/Huey_Long.md) — U.S. Senator and former Louisiana governor poised to challenge FDR in 1936. Shot at the Louisiana State Capitol; alleged assassin Dr. Carl Weiss killed on the spot by bodyguards firing 30–60+ rounds. No autopsy, no inquest, evidence missing. Predates the modern intelligence community; included for political-assassination context.

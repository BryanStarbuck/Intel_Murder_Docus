## Why This Group Exists Separately

This investigation documents deaths and suppression connected to Jeffrey Epstein's trafficking and blackmail operation. Hollywood is a **different network with the same shape.**

The distinction matters and is easy to lose. Accounts of entertainment industry abuse frequently involve yachts, private parties, powerful older men, and teenage victims brought in by a trusted intermediary — the same visual grammar as the Epstein case. That resemblance causes unrelated allegations to be absorbed into Epstein narratives online, which damages both stories: it attaches unfounded claims to the Epstein record, and it buries genuine industry allegations under a conspiracy frame that mainstream outlets can then dismiss wholesale.

So this page keeps them apart. **Where a person's connection is to the entertainment industry and not to Epstein, that is stated explicitly on their profile.** [Lou Pearlman](../../Details/Lou_Pearlman.mdx) and [Hayden Panettiere](../../Details/Hayden_Panettiere.mdx) are both documented here with an affirmative statement that no Epstein connection is reported.

What the two networks share is the mechanism:

* **A supply of minors with legitimate reasons to be present** — auditions, sets, tours, agency contracts — rather than abduction.
* **An intermediary the victim trusts.** In Epstein's operation, recruiters. In the industry accounts, managers, agents, "friends," and in several cases the victim's own parent.
* **Institutional incentive to suppress.** Studios, labels, and networks carry financial exposure when talent is implicated; the same reflex that protects a production protects the accused.
* **Discrediting rather than denial.** Accusers are characterized as unstable, addicted, or opportunistic — often using addictions the industry itself is alleged to have started.
* **Documentation that does not reach the public.** *An Open Secret* (2014) could not secure distribution. Footage of accusations has been destroyed. Memoirs are published only after statutes of limitation have expired.

## Alleged Activities

*All items below are allegations unless noted as court-proven. Where individuals are unnamed in the original accounts, this site does not speculate about their identity.*

* Sexual abuse of minors on sets, at auditions, and at private industry parties
* Grooming of child performers, including administering stimulants and sedatives to minors
* Use of yachts, private residences, and parties as venues insulated from oversight
* Retaliation against performers who disclose abuse — lost contracts, blacklisting, public mockery
* Suppression of documentary evidence, including destroyed broadcast footage and blocked distribution
* Financial exploitation of child performers by managers, agents, and family members

## Key Figures and Cases

### Documented in this investigation

* **[Hayden Panettiere](../../Details/Hayden_Panettiere.mdx)** — Published *This Is Me: A Reckoning* in May 2026 alleging she was groomed as a child performer, given what she believed were amphetamines at approximately 15 by her own team, and assaulted at 18 (on a yacht, by an unnamed musician) and at 19 (by an unnamed Oscar-winning actor and director). She named no one. She was found dead at 36 on August 16, 2026, 97 days after the accompanying podcast interview. **Cause of death pending; police report no signs of foul play. No Epstein connection reported.**
* **[Corey Haim](../../Details/Corey_Haim.md)** — Child star who described being raped at 13 on a film set in 1986. Died at 38 in 2010 of pneumonia following years of substance abuse. No one was ever charged.
* **[Isaac Kappy](../../Details/Isaac_Kappy.md)** — Actor who spent a year publicly naming figures he accused of abuse. Died May 13, 2019 after going off an Arizona bridge into traffic; ruled suicide.
* **[Alexis Arquette](../../Details/Alexis_Arquette.md)** — Described her early career as a "tornado of cocaine and pedophilia" and was reportedly planning a tell-all memoir. Died September 11, 2016 of HIV-related cardiac arrest — medically explained, but she died before the memoir was published.
* **[Mark Salling](../../Details/Mark_Salling.md)** — *Glee* actor found with more than 50,000 child abuse images and an instruction manual. Hanged himself in January 2018 before sentencing. **The distribution network that supplied his collection was never traced.**
* **[Lou Pearlman](../../Details/Lou_Pearlman.mdx)** — Boy band mogul who created the Backstreet Boys and NSYNC, accused by multiple former band members of sexually abusing boys he managed. Convicted of one of the longest Ponzi schemes in American history; died in federal custody in 2016 with the alleged abuse ring never investigated to resolution. **A parallel ring, not part of the Epstein network.**
* **[Karen Mulder](../../Details/Karen_Mulder.md)** — Supermodel who named two [Elite Model Management](elite_model_management.md) executives as rapists on French television in 2001. The footage was destroyed and she was hospitalized. Illustrates the destroyed-documentation pattern.

### Public figures who have spoken about industry abuse (living, not accused of anything)

* **Corey Feldman** — *The Goonies*, *Stand by Me*. Has described sexual abuse of child actors for decades, in his memoir *Coreyography* (2013) and the 2020 documentary *My Truth: The Rape of 2 Coreys*. Publicly mocked on national television for years before the #MeToo era.
* **Elijah Wood** — Said in a 2016 *Sunday Times* interview that there are "a lot of vipers in this industry." He later clarified he had no first-hand knowledge and was referencing *An Open Secret*.
* **Jennette McCurdy** — *I'm Glad My Mom Died* (2022), on maternal abuse and industry pressure on child performers.
* **Stephen Baldwin** — Said on Fox Business in 2017 that Hollywood harassment scandals were "the tip of the iceberg, because in the culture and the industry of Hollywood this has been pervasive." *This is general industry commentary. Baldwin has said nothing about Hayden Panettiere, despite a widely circulated August 2026 social media post implying otherwise.*

## Connection to the Epstein Network

**Direct overlap is limited and should not be overstated.** The genuine points of contact run through the **modeling industry**, not through film, television, or music:

* [Elite Model Management](elite_model_management.md) and [MC2 Model Management](mc2_model_management.md) are documented pipelines into Epstein's operation, and both operated at the intersection of fashion and entertainment.
* [Ruslana Korshunova](../../Details/Ruslana_Korshunova.md) and [Nadia Marcinko](../../Details/Nadia_Marcinko.md) entered Epstein's orbit through modeling.
* [Bohemian Grove](bohemian_grove.md) draws attendees from entertainment as well as politics and finance.

Beyond those, the Hollywood cases documented here are **their own network**. Corey Haim, Mark Salling, Lou Pearlman, and Hayden Panettiere have no reported connection to Epstein. Treating them as Epstein cases would be inaccurate.

## Why This Group Matters

The Epstein case is often framed as an aberration — one financier with unusual access and unusual protection. The Hollywood record suggests the opposite: that the structure is **reproducible wherever an industry controls access to minors and has financial reason to protect the adults around them.** Epstein's operation had an alleged intelligence dimension that Hollywood's does not. What it did not have that Hollywood does is a legitimate, continuous, publicly celebrated supply of children whose parents deliver them voluntarily.

The pattern is also its own counterargument. Child performers die young at a rate that requires no murder theory to be damning: addiction started by adults on their payroll, no aftercare when the work ends, and a discrediting apparatus that activates the moment they speak. *Variety* made precisely this argument in August 2026 in a piece headlined "Hollywood Failed Hayden Panettiere." **The documented, defensible indictment is negligence and protection at scale — not assassination.** This site does not claim otherwise, and marks clearly where evidence stops.

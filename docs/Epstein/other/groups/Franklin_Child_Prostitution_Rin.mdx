The operational mechanics of the 1980s Omaha trafficking network: how minors were recruited, conditioned through alleged trauma-based mind control, and turned into intelligence-linked "assets" exploited by powerful men.

## Overview

The Franklin Child Prostitution Ring refers specifically to the trafficking and exploitation operation allegedly run through and around the Franklin Community Federal Credit Union in Omaha, Nebraska, by its manager Lawrence E. "Larry" King Jr. beginning in the late 1970s or early 1980s. While the [Franklin Scandal overview](franklin_scandal.mdx) documents the broader political cover-up, this page focuses on the operational structure of the ring itself: how children were recruited, conditioned, and systematically exploited.

According to multiple accusers — including [Paul Bonacci](#paul-bonacci-survivor-profile), Alisha Owen, and others — King and his associates recruited vulnerable children primarily from two sources: the Boys Town Catholic institution for at-risk youth in Omaha and Nebraska's foster care system. Once recruited, children were allegedly subjected to trauma, drugging, and conditioning before being trafficked to elite parties in multiple cities.

A federal civil court, in awarding Paul Bonacci a $1 million default judgment in 1999, determined that Bonacci's submitted testimony was credible enough to establish civil liability. Lawrence King did not appear to contest the case.

## How Children Were Turned Into "Assets"

According to survivor testimony and investigative reporting, the ring operated as a structured exploitation pipeline:

* **Recruitment** — Children were sourced primarily from Boys Town and the Nebraska foster care system; vulnerable youth with troubled backgrounds and few family advocates were targeted
* **Grooming and conditioning** — Accusers described a process of systematic abuse beginning at young ages, designed to ensure compliance and create dependence
* **Trauma-based programming** — Multiple survivors alleged exposure to techniques consistent with what researchers and activists describe as trauma-based mind control (see Project Monarch section below); testimony described deliberate psychological fragmentation
* **Trafficking to parties** — Children were allegedly flown on private planes to cities including Chicago, New York, and Washington DC, and presented at parties hosted for powerful men including politicians and businessmen
* **Use as couriers** — Some accusers alleged they were also used to transport materials, including potentially compromising materials, between powerful figures
* **Photographic/video documentation** — Accusers alleged that abuse was photographed or filmed, potentially for use as blackmail material against participants; Gary Caradori reportedly obtained such photographs before his death

This systematic approach — recruitment from institutions, conditioning, trafficking, documentation — mirrors patterns documented in later investigations of elite trafficking networks, most notably that of Jeffrey Epstein.

## Paul Bonacci: Survivor Profile {#paul-bonacci-survivor-profile}

Paul Bonacci is the most extensively documented survivor-witness of the Franklin ring. According to his own testimony and court filings:

* Bonacci stated he was first abused within the Franklin network at approximately age 8, recruited from the Omaha area
* As a teenager and young adult, he was used in multiple capacities within the alleged ring: as a victim at parties, as a courier, and allegedly as a participant in the production of child pornography
* In a video deposition recorded when he was approximately 22 years old, Bonacci described in detail the structure of the ring, the people involved, and the places he was taken — including cross-country trips and what he alleged was a ritualistic event at Bohemian Grove
* He was charged with perjury by the Nebraska grand jury but his case took a different course than that of Alisha Owen; he was not ultimately convicted on those charges
* On **February 1, 1991**, former Nebraska State Senator John DeCamp filed a federal civil suit on Bonacci's behalf against Lawrence King and others
* On **February 4, 1999**, U.S. District Judge Warren Urbom entered a default judgment in Bonacci's favor: **$800,000 in compensatory damages and $200,000 in punitive damages** — $1 million total. King did not appear to defend the case. The judgment was never paid
* Bonacci's testimony is archived in court documents and referenced extensively in DeCamp's *The Franklin Cover-Up* and Nick Bryant's *The Franklin Scandal*

**Paul Bonacci on Michael Aquino:** In sworn testimony, Bonacci identified Lt. Col. Michael A. Aquino — a U.S. Army psychological warfare specialist and founder of the Temple of Set — as a man he knew only as "the Colonel," whom he alleged was a senior figure in the exploitation network. Bonacci also identified Aquino in connection with the 1982 kidnapping of Johnny Gosch, a 12-year-old Des Moines newspaper delivery boy who disappeared and was never officially found. Aquino denied these allegations; he was investigated by U.S. Army Criminal Investigation Command in connection with the Presidio Child Development Center abuse case in the late 1980s and was not charged. Aquino died on September 1, 2019.

**Bohemian Grove testimony:** According to DeCamp's book and Bonacci's depositions, Bonacci alleged he was taken to Bohemian Grove — the all-male elite retreat in Monte Rio, California — where he claimed to witness the murder of a young boy during a ritualistic event. No physical evidence of this claim has been produced, and it is among the most contested elements of Bonacci's testimony.

**Status:** As of 2026, Paul Bonacci's current whereabouts and status are not publicly confirmed. He was alive and gave public interviews as recently as the early 2000s in connection with the Johnny Gosch case.

## Project Monarch and MK Ultra: The Alleged Programming Connection

"Project Monarch" is the name applied by researchers, survivors, and activists to an alleged trauma-based mind control sub-program operating under or parallel to the CIA's documented MK Ultra program (which ran from the early 1950s until officially terminated in 1973). The existence of Monarch as a formally named program has not been confirmed by declassified documents, but its alleged mechanics are consistent with what was documented in official MK Ultra files.

Paul Bonacci is among the best-known individuals who claimed to have been subjected to Monarch-type programming. According to his testimony and interviews:

* He described the deliberate use of trauma — physical, sexual, and psychological — to fragment his personality and create what he described as multiple controllable identities or "alters"
* Each "alter" could allegedly be triggered, deployed for a specific purpose, and then suppressed, leaving the primary personality with no conscious memory of what occurred
* He described being conditioned by multiple handlers over years, with the goal of creating an obedient, programmable human "asset"
* He identified Michael Aquino as one of the figures involved in his programming, describing Aquino's role in psychological conditioning and his connection to Satanic ritual elements
* Ted Gunderson, former FBI Special Agent in Charge of the Los Angeles field office, investigated these claims and stated in multiple public presentations that Monarch-type programming was real and that Bonacci's testimony was consistent with what he had learned from other sources

**MK Ultra foundation:** The CIA's MK Ultra program — documented through thousands of pages released via FOIA — included experiments in hypnosis, drug-induced regression, sensory deprivation, electroconvulsive therapy, and psychological conditioning. The program targeted children in some documented cases. The Church Committee (1975) and subsequent congressional investigations revealed that MK Ultra was far more extensive than initially disclosed. Whether Monarch was a continuation, a separate program, or an informal operational practice drawing on MK Ultra techniques is disputed.

**Defamation note:** Claims about Project Monarch and the application of these techniques to child trafficking have not been adjudicated in court. They are documented here as sworn testimony and investigative reporting. They represent one framework for understanding survivor accounts — not a court-proven fact.

## Intelligence Connections

The ring's alleged intelligence dimensions are among the most contested and significant aspects of the case:

* **CIA involvement alleged:** Multiple investigators, including Ted Gunderson (former FBI SAC), alleged that elements of the U.S. intelligence community had knowledge of and possibly operational interest in the Franklin ring — whether as a blackmail operation targeting politicians or as a continuation of mind control programs
* **Michael Aquino / psychological warfare:** Aquino's documented role as a U.S. Army psychological warfare specialist lends a degree of institutional credibility to the allegation that his alleged involvement in the ring had intelligence dimensions beyond personal criminal conduct
* **The DC connection:** The alleged trafficking of children to Washington DC parties, and the overlap with Craig Spence's operation (which had documented CIA connections), suggests the Franklin ring fed into a broader Beltway-level Compromat operation
* **Cover-up structure:** The FBI's alleged failure to investigate, the grand jury finding of "hoax," and the prosecutorial inversion — jailing accusers rather than alleged perpetrators — are consistent with the pattern seen when intelligence-connected operations face exposure

## Key Figures

* **Lawrence E. "Larry" King Jr.** — Convicted embezzler; alleged ring operator; denied all sex trafficking allegations; never charged with a sex crime; believed living; all trafficking/abuse claims are attributed allegations not proven in court
* **Paul Bonacci** — Survivor; won $1 million civil default judgment (1999); alleged victim of Project Monarch programming; identified multiple senior figures including Aquino in sworn testimony
* **Alisha Owen** — Survivor who refused to recant her accusations; convicted of eight perjury counts by the Nebraska grand jury; served 4.5 years in prison
* **Troy Boner** — Survivor who recanted under pressure; died in 2003 under suspicious circumstances (found bleeding from the mouth in a New Mexico hospital after screaming that people were after him)
* **Aaron Owen** — 17-year-old brother of Alisha Owen; found hanged in his prison cell shortly before Alisha's perjury trial (November 1990); ruled suicide; death widely questioned
* **Lt. Col. Michael A. Aquino** — U.S. Army psychological warfare officer; founder of the Temple of Set; identified by Bonacci as "the Colonel" and alleged senior figure in the ring and in Monarch programming; denied all allegations; investigated but not charged in the Presidio daycare case; **deceased September 1, 2019**
* **[John DeCamp](../../../Details/John_DeCamp.mdx)** — Nebraska state senator and attorney; represented Bonacci; wrote *The Franklin Cover-Up*; **deceased May 2, 2017**
* **[Gary Caradori](../../../Details/Gary_Caradori.mdx)** — Nebraska Legislature's private investigator; reportedly obtained photographic evidence of ring activities; **killed in mid-air plane disintegration July 11, 1990**; his briefcase of evidence was never recovered
* **[Ted Gunderson](../../../Details/Ted_Gunderson.mdx)** — Former FBI SAC; investigated Franklin and Monarch allegations; publicly supported Bonacci's testimony; **deceased July 31, 2011**
* **Johnny Gosch** — 12-year-old newspaper delivery boy abducted in Des Moines, Iowa, September 5, 1982; Bonacci identified himself as a participant in the kidnapping under duress and named Aquino as the organizer; never officially found; case remains open

## Connection to Epstein Network

The structural parallels between the Franklin ring and the Epstein operation are extensively documented in Whitney Webb's *One Nation Under Blackmail*:

* Both operations recruited young people, many from vulnerable populations
* Both had documented intelligence service connections
* Both operated as blackmail platforms capturing powerful men in compromising situations
* Both featured systematic suppression of investigations and witness intimidation
* Both involved the disappearance or destruction of photographic/video evidence
* Both had investigators who died under suspicious circumstances during active investigations
* Epstein's operation was more sophisticated and global; the Franklin ring appears to be a domestic predecessor that demonstrated the model worked

The cover-up of the Franklin ring — which resulted in the accusers being jailed for perjury while no one was convicted of trafficking — may have provided a template for how subsequent elite trafficking operations could be shielded from prosecution.

## Why This Page Matters

The [Franklin Scandal overview](franklin_scandal.mdx) documents the political dimensions — the cover-up, the investigator deaths, the media suppression. * The same operational template — vulnerable children, institutional recruitment, trauma conditioning, elite trafficking, blackmail documentation — appears in subsequent operations

## Assessment: HIGHLY SUSPICIOUS

Manuel Schadwald's disappearance is a documented, unsolved fact: a twelve-year-old left his mother's apartment in Berlin-Tempelhof on 24 July 1993 to travel across the city to a children's leisure centre, never arrived, and has not been seen since. What makes the case belong in this investigation is not the disappearance itself but everything that happened around the search for him. Within two years, leads pursued by Dutch and Belgian journalists and activists pointed to brothels in Amsterdam and Rotterdam. By 1998 the file had led investigators into the Zandvoort collection of child abuse material — and the Belgian welfare officer assigned to the Schadwald file, [Gina Pardaens](Gina_Pardaens.md), was dead within four months of taking it on, her car driven into a bridge pillar with no brake marks the day after her house was burgled. The German man named as the source of the first Zandvoort CD-ROM, Gerrit Ulrich, had been shot dead in Italy weeks earlier. The Berlin police closed the case in 1998 without result.

The gravest allegation attached to the case — that Manuel was killed aboard a yacht while the killing was filmed — comes from activists and from claimed intelligence material, **has never been confirmed by any court, inquest, or published official document, and is repeated here only as an allegation with its source named**.

## Circumstances of the Disappearance

Manuel Schadwald was born in West Berlin on 24 January 1981 and lived with his mother in the Tempelhof district. On the afternoon of **24 July 1993**, he left the apartment alone to travel by public transport to the **FEZ Wuhlheide**, a large children's and youth leisure centre in Berlin-Köpenick, on the far side of the city. He was wearing jeans, black trainers, a grey T-shirt and a grey jacket, and carrying a turquoise backpack.

He never arrived. He was reported missing and the case drew heavy German press coverage and a substantial police investigation. No trace of him — no body, no clothing, no confirmed sighting after that afternoon — has ever been recovered.

## The Netherlands Leads

The reason this case sits in the Netherlands group rather than in a German missing-persons file is that every substantive lead ran west across the Dutch border.

* **Rotterdam and Amsterdam brothels.** According to journalism cited on the case's public record, Dutch reporters traced Manuel to a Rotterdam brothel said to be operated by Lothar Glandorf, and police surveillance logs from 1993–1994 recorded a boy matching his description in Glandorf's company. Dutch investigations in both cities were discontinued in 1995 without result.
* **The Zandvoort material.** In 1998 the Belgian group Werkgroep Morkhoven handed police tens of thousands of images and videos traced to Zandvoort. Activists working the Schadwald file said Manuel appeared in material connected to that collection. **Dutch and German police did not confirm any identification**, and Berlin police had already denied the 1997 Dutch television report.
* **Gerrit Ulrich.** Ulrich, a German living in the Netherlands, was named by the activists as the source of the first Zandvoort CD-ROM. According to reporting summarized on the case's Wikipedia entry, one of Ulrich's associates told journalists that he had brought Manuel from Berlin to the Netherlands. Ulrich was shot dead in Pisa in June 1998, before the file became public; Dutch authorities pursued a named Belgian suspect over the killing.
* **The Dutroux overlap.** The same activists, in the same period, were working the [Dutroux Affair](../other/groups/dutroux_affair.md) in Belgium, in which at least 27 people connected to the investigation died. Whether the two networks were genuinely one network, or two contemporaneous cases worked by the same small group of investigators, has never been established by any court.

## The Yacht Apollo Allegation — Unverified

The most widely circulated claim about Manuel Schadwald is that he was raped and killed aboard a sailing yacht named **Apollo** while the killing was filmed, that his body was disposed of at sea, and that the vessel was afterwards cleaned at a Dutch naval port because "influential people were on board."

**This allegation is unverified.** Its sourcing, as best it can be traced:

* Claimed **Dutch intelligence material** said to record the murder of a German boy on a Dutch boat and a cover-up because of who was aboard. No such document has been published, authenticated, or produced in any proceeding.
* **[Gina Pardaens](Gina_Pardaens.md)** stated that she possessed a copy of a film showing a child's murder and that she recognized a close associate of Michel Nihoul among the participants. She died on 15 November 1998. The copy she described has never surfaced.
* The yacht is said to have been owned by a Dutch accountant whose associate was **Gerrit Ulrich**. Ulrich was dead before the file became public and never answered any question about it.

No court, coroner, or public inquiry in the Netherlands or Germany has made any finding on the Apollo claim. The Dutch police stated they found no organized network in the Zandvoort material. Every element of this allegation should be read as an allegation.

## The Father

Manuel's father, Rainer Wolf, came under police suspicion during the investigation and, according to press reports, was arrested in 1998 and released after several days without charge. *Berliner Morgenpost* reported claims that he had worked as a Stasi agent from 1984 and, according to intelligence-linked sources cited in that reporting, had been involved in blackmail using child abuse material. **He was never charged in connection with his son's disappearance, and these are reported allegations rather than findings of any court.** He has not, so far as the public record shows, responded publicly to them.

## Why This Case Raises Questions

- A twelve-year-old vanished in a major European capital in broad daylight and the case was closed five years later with no body, no suspect, and no finding
- German press reported at the time that investigating authorities had not pursued the leads sufficiently or intensively
- The leads that did exist all pointed across a border, into a jurisdiction whose own investigations were discontinued in 1995
- The Belgian welfare officer assigned to his file died four months later in a single-vehicle crash with no brake marks, the day after her house was burgled
- The man named as the source of the first Zandvoort CD-ROM was shot dead weeks before the material became public
- Dutch police examined tens of thousands of images of child abuse and reported they could identify neither the children nor an organized network
- The activist who handed over the material was himself later prosecuted and convicted in Belgium
- 2015 reporting in *Die Welt* and *Algemeen Dagblad* indicated the Berlin prosecutor's office had information as early as 1993 that abducted Berlin children were being sold to Benelux brothels

## The Counterargument

* Children do disappear without a trafficking network behind it, and a 1993 disappearance in a large city with no witnesses and no forensic trace is genuinely difficult to solve
* Berlin police explicitly **denied** that Manuel had been identified in an abuse film after the 1997 Dutch television report; no identification has ever been confirmed
* Dutch police assessments described the Zandvoort links to Schadwald as **circumstantial**
* The most dramatic elements of the story — the yacht, the naval port, the intelligence files, the influential passengers — rest on activist accounts and claimed documents that have never been produced
* The story has circulated for nearly three decades on advocacy sites and social media and has visibly accumulated detail along the way; the 2026 X thread that prompted this profile states the yacht killing as established fact, which the public record does not support
* Marcel Vervloesem, the source of the Zandvoort material, was convicted in a Belgian court in 2008 on child sex abuse charges — a fact that is either the retaliation his supporters describe or a legitimate reason for caution, and the site does not claim to know which
